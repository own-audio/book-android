# Privacy

The app collects nothing. There is no account with us, no analytics, no ads, no crash
reporting and no tracking.

It talks only to:

- **the servers you add**: your sign-in, requests for your library, the books and covers you
  play, your positions, bookmarks and favorites, and books you import or ask to be narrated;
- **Apple**, only if you choose **Continue with Apple** for an own.audio server. Apple's page
  opens in your browser; the app gets nothing from Apple directly, the own.audio server does;
- **Google**, only if you choose **Continue with Google** for an own.audio server. This is in
  the Google Play build only; the F-Droid build has no Google code.

What a server does with that is up to whoever runs it: you, a friend, or a hosting service with
its own policy.

On the phone the app keeps your servers and sign-ins (in one file encrypted with a key kept in
Android's Keystore; sign-in tokens only, never a password), your settings and positions,
downloaded books, covers, and saved answers from your servers so it works offline. None of it
goes into Android's cloud backup. Uninstalling the app removes all of it; removing a server
removes its downloads and what the app kept for it.

The **Logs** in Settings stay on the phone unless you share them yourself.

The full policy: [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/own-audio/book-android/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
