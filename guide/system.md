# Android Auto, the widgets and the notification
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## The notification and the lock screen

While a book plays, the app shows a media notification, also on the lock screen. It has the
cover, title and author, play/pause, and skip back and skip forward by your intervals from
[Settings](settings.md#playback). Its extra buttons change the **Speed** (0.75×, 1×, 1.25×,
1.5×, 1.75×, 2×, then back to 0.75×) and, in a book with chapters, go to the previous or next
chapter.

The book keeps playing with the app in the background or the screen off.

## Headphones and Bluetooth

Play, pause and skip work from headphones, a Bluetooth speaker or the car. Pressing play on a
headset or in the system's media controls after the app was closed resumes the book you were
listening to.

## Widgets

own.book has three home-screen widgets. They take the colors of your wallpaper (Android 12 and
later), with the play button and the progress in the app's color, and follow light and dark mode.

- **Player** (4×1): the cover, the book and the author, with skip back, **Play**/**Pause** and
  skip forward, by the intervals you chose in Settings. Tap the book to open the player. On a
  narrow strip only **Play**/**Pause** stays; stretched taller, it becomes the large player.
- **Now Playing** (4×2): a large cover, the book, the author, the time left, how far you are, and
  the same buttons. When no book is in the player, it shows **Continue** with the book you were
  listening to and how much of it you have heard; **Play** goes on with it.
- **Continue Listening** (4×2): the books you are listening to as covers, each with a thin line
  for how far you are, as many as fit the widget's size. Tap one to open the app and play it.

The buttons work without opening the app. While a book plays, the progress moves on about once a
minute. To add a widget, touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen, tap **Widgets**, and look
for own.book. A Continue Listening widget you added before this version becomes the new one.

## Shortcut

Touch and hold the own.book icon on the home screen for the **Continue Listening** shortcut. It
goes on with the book you were listening to. You can drag the shortcut onto the home screen.

## Google Assistant

The app answers media requests from the Assistant, for example:

- "Continue listening on own.book", "Continue my book on own.book": goes on with the book you
  were listening to.
- "Play *book title* on own.book": plays a book from the selected server, from where you left
  off. Offline, it looks among your downloads.

## Android Auto

The app works with Android Auto, but it has not been tried on a real car screen yet, so it may
not work exactly as described.

It lists **Continue Listening**, **Downloads**, **Library**, **Series** and **Authors** for the
selected server (Continue Listening and Downloads come first, because a car often has no
signal), and you can search for a book. While a book plays, its chapters are listed, and a
speed button steps 0.75× → 1× → 1.25× → 1.5× → 1.75× → 2× and back. In the car the app never
asks which device's position to use; it takes the newest. With no server added, it says "Add a
server on your phone".
