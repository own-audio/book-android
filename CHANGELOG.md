# Changelog

All notable changes to own.audio Books for Android. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

- Home-screen widgets redesigned: three instead of one. **Player** (4×1: cover, book, author, skip back, play/pause, skip forward by the listener's intervals), **Now Playing** (4×2: large cover, time left, progress and the same buttons; **Continue · 42%** with the book under way when nothing is in the player) and **Continue Listening** (4×2: the books in progress as a grid of covers with a thin progress line each, filling the widget's size). Wallpaper colours (Material You) with the play button and progress in the app's colour, the launcher's corner radius, 14–16 sp titles, 48 dp buttons, responsive sizes, picker previews (a preview layout, an image below Android 12, generated previews from Android 15) and English and Czech descriptions. The buttons work without opening the app: play goes to the media service as the notification's does (and resumes the last book), pause and the skips go through a media controller and the session's skip commands. The players are redrawn on play, pause and another book and once a minute while playing. A Continue Listening widget placed before keeps working as the new one. Adds `glance-material3` and, for tests, `glance-appwidget-testing` (docs/dependencies.md).
- Sign in with Apple, in both builds: **Continue with Apple** on the own.audio account sheet, under the server form and on Sign In Again, wherever the own.audio server runs Apple's sign-in in the browser (discovery `auth.apple_web`, or the apple-code grant). It opens the server's page in a Custom Tab and comes back by `audio.own.bookplayer:/apple` with a one-time code, tied to the app with PKCE and a `state`; a sign-in waiting for the browser survives the app being ended. Adds AndroidX Browser. docs/android-foundation.md §10.4 changed in music-android, copied here.
- A server that didn't answer (a NAS restarting, no DNS yet at launch) is asked again while the app is on screen — after 15 s, 30 s, 1 min, 2 min, then every 5 min, and at once on coming back to the app or opening its page — instead of staying "Unreachable" until the network changed or Check Now. Nothing is asked in the background, with the screen locked, offline, or of a server that answers.
- Every swipe acts once and the row closes again (downloads, bookmarks both ways, and the servers in Settings, whose rows are now keyed by server): a check on the row's swiped state could act again and again while the row stayed open, as it did in own.audio Podcasts.
- Fixes from the emulator test against real servers (docs/tests/2026-11-22-emulator.md): the book page shows a playing file's own chapter marks, the player's tools stay reachable on short phones, bookmarks and progress on the book page follow the player, Back on a tab's first screen goes to Home, Whole Library When Offline reloads the Library, Audiobookshelf series without a number and bookmarks without a label, a second swipe in Downloads, tab labels at 200 % text.
- Release preparation: a release workflow for tags (both flavours' APKs and the Play bundle, signed when the repository has the key), the guide published by GitHub Pages, store texts in English and Czech with the 512 px icon (also F-Droid's layout), the APK size budget per app, and docs/release.md with the maintainer's steps.
- Adding an account the app already has (the same server and the same user) is refused with "This server is already added. To sign in again, open it in Settings."; another user on the same server can still be added.
- B13: TalkBack names and values throughout, headings, 48 dp targets, layouts at 200 % text, the player side by side on tablets in landscape, phones upright; removing a server stops its book; a finished download replaces the stream through the spoken player's own swap; misuse tests.
- The user guide (`guide/`, for GitHub Pages): every screen, with screenshots.
- A book or episode loaded to be shown paused no longer starts playing by itself after the previous one played (the shared spoken player).
- B12: Android Auto (Continue Listening, Downloads, Library, Series, Authors, the chapters while a book plays, a speed button, search), the Continue Listening widget, ownbook://play links, the assistant ("continue listening", "continue my book", "play ‹book›") and a Continue Listening shortcut; Play on a headset or the system's controls after the app was ended resumes the last book.
- The spoken player takes a part's real length from the file when the stated one is missing or wrong, accepts chapters that arrive after loading, and switches to a downloaded copy at the next pause or seek without losing the place.
- Narrate an Ebook sends the same Idempotency-Key for every attempt at the same request: a retry after a lost answer never starts a second paid job.
- B10 and B11: Import a Book from audio files or a folder on this device (tags fill the title, author and cover; the order can be changed; the upload survives the app being killed and a retry never makes the book twice), and Narrate an Ebook on own.audio's hosted service (language, voice, files, the price first). Importing and Narrating rows in the Library.
- B6 and B9: downloads (a book, or a whole series, author, narrator, collection or filtered list), the Downloads tab, Keep at Most by whole books (finished ones first, never the playing one), a download replacing the stream at the next pause, Offline Mode with the offline banner, the library from downloads (or the whole own.audio library) when offline, live updates while the app is open, an hourly refresh, and the Downloads and offline settings; skip intervals and speed for new books follow the own.audio account. Audiobookshelf descriptions show plain text instead of HTML entities.
- B7 and B8: positions are never lost (kept on disk, merged, sent at once on pause, seek or chapter and otherwise once a minute, batched when several wait), "Continue from ‹device›?" when another device got further, another device's newer position taken on own.audio; bookmarks (add, rename, delete; Audiobookshelf by time), favorites, Mark as Finished / Not Finished, Reset Progress, Share with the Family / Keep to Myself, and covers that load a fresh link once when theirs has expired.
- `:core:downloads` and `:core:sync` (download workers and index, outboxes, background refresh, live updates), copied from music-android.
- B3 and B4: the spoken player on Media3 and the book screens — Home with shelves, Library (series, authors, narrators, collections; sort, filter, grid or list), the book page with chapters, files and bookmarks, Search, the mini player and the player (chapter slider, speed, sleep timer, chapters); plays in the background with skip and chapter buttons on the notification; positions written to disk before they are sent. Players now wait through long signal gaps instead of failing after ten minutes.
- B5 and the app shell, ported from music-android: welcome, own.audio account, Try the Demo Library, Add Server for own.audio and Audiobookshelf (book libraries only, a library picker, the server's own icon), Settings, the server page, Family, Devices, Listening and Logs. A privacy policy (PRIVACY.md).
- `:player:core` with the Media3 playback pieces (link resolver, data source chain, listening cache, session callback, skip commands), copied from music-android.
- B2: the book library — one model over own.audio and Audiobookshelf, own.audio's catalogue on the phone (one Room database per server, fed by the sync snapshot and change feed; collections too), Audiobookshelf through its pages and shelves with one play session per book, the resume choice and the download eviction rules.
- The spoken engine's logic (B3, part): one timeline across a book's files, chapter navigation, chapters read from the files themselves (MP4/M4B chapter tracks and Nero `chpl`, MP3 ID3 `CHAP`/`CTOC`, FLAC and Ogg comments) within 6 seconds, smart rewind, the end-of-chapter and end-of-book sleep modes, speed steps and their text.
- `:player:core` (sleep timer, resume store, storage route, payload check), copied from music-android.
- `:core:servers`, copied from music-android (F3).
- `:kit:ownaudio`, the own.audio /api/v2 client, copied from music-android (F2).
- F1's look from music-android with the brown accent, the book words and the book icon.
- `:kit:audiobookshelf` (B1): the Audiobookshelf client with rotating tokens, play sessions, progress, bookmarks and podcast libraries.
- The build skeleton from music-android's F0: both flavours, the module layout, four empty tabs in English and Czech, CI, the drift check.
- Implementation plan and the shared Android foundation document.
